Privacy Policy

Freedom Exteriors CRM · Last updated October 3, 2026

The Freedom Exteriors CRM (the "App") is internal software used only by Freedom Exteriors LLC ("we") to manage our own customers and jobs.

What we store

Customer and job details our staff enter (names, phone numbers, email and job addresses, estimates, invoices, photos and notes), and the login used to connect our own QuickBooks Online company.

QuickBooks data

With our administrator's authorization, the App reads our QuickBooks company information and creates customers, invoices and payments in our own QuickBooks company. QuickBooks access tokens are kept only on our servers, never in a web browser, and are deleted when QuickBooks is disconnected.

How it's protected and shared

Data is stored with our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Supabase) over encrypted connections, behind staff logins. We do not sell or share this information with anyone else, and use it only to serve our customers.

Your choices

Customers may ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about them by contacting us below.
